# Prompt B, round 2 — Cool Athlete Coach

Create a polished product-design contact sheet for **Nudge**, using the complete shared
brief below.

Concept B is the **Cool Athlete Coach**: smooth in every way, fluent with the latest
training technology, and quietly confident in a useful workout recommendation while the
user's choice remains final. Express that personality through effortless pacing and
action-first hierarchy, not motivational copy or a celebrity persona. The interface
should feel premium, athletic, and unusually composed: decisive scale changes, generous
negative space, continuous horizontal bands or layers, and controls that seem to arrive
exactly where the hand or eye needs them.

Give this concept a recognizable composition: a bold current-action zone, secondary data
visually quieter in peripheral regions, dynamic asymmetry, and a strong sense of forward
motion without arrows, racing graphics, or animation-dependent meaning. Every supplied
datum must remain visible—never hidden, collapsed, or omitted. Performance should be the
clearest and fastest of the three concepts to operate between sets. Planning should make
the prepared workout feel immediate. Review and History should feel composed and
confident rather than administrative.

Use advanced-tech restraint. Do not use neon, glassmorphism, generic gradients, sci-fi
HUD decoration, oversized lifestyle photography, motivational slogans, or a hamburger
menu. “Cool” comes from proportion, rhythm, restraint, and responsiveness—not spectacle.

Preserve one obvious primary action and state hierarchy in each frame. Treat numbers,
timers, targets, recommendations, and phase comparisons as primary material. Use upright
typography and tabular numerals where useful. Keep text legible and controls plausible.
At mobile width, avoid horizontal scrolling and preserve at least 44 × 44 px targets.
Show visible keyboard focus, strong contrast, and non-color status cues in at least one
frame. Meaning must survive reduced motion.

Do not add readiness scores, streaks, calories, heart rate, recovery claims,
achievements, invented metrics, new navigation, a Journal route, or coaching commands.
Avoid generic gradients, glass panels, neon, decorative metrics, bodybuilding motifs,
racing italics, excessive cards, guilt, hype, fake urgency, and simulated intimacy.

This must be a fundamentally different interface grammar, not the existing interface
with a new tint or accent color. Avoid default header-plus-cards dashboard composition.
If the logo is hidden and the image is converted to grayscale, this concept must remain
recognizable as the Cool Athlete Coach through composition, typography, density, scale,
and information choreography.
